To make part of a picture transparent

A picture can have see-through areas, like the background of a logo or a sticker.

  1. On the Image menu, click Attributes.
  2. Under Transparency, click Transparent, and then click OK.
  3. Erase the parts you want to be see-through. In a transparent picture, the Eraser makes areas transparent instead of painting them with the background color, and shows a moving rainbow while you erase, since "invisible" is hard to preview.

Notes

  • In a transparent picture, Clear Image on the Image menu makes the whole picture transparent, and moving a selection leaves a transparent hole behind it.
  • To keep the transparency, save the picture as a PNG or GIF file. Formats without transparency, such as JPEG, fill the transparent areas in.
  • When you open a picture that already has transparent areas, Paint turns transparency on by itself.
  • This isn't the same as Draw Opaque on the Image menu, which decides whether a selection's background color covers what's under it. Paint has more than one kind of transparency, which is either thorough or confusing.
