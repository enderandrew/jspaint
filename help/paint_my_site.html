To manage your site's pages and files

  1. On the My Site menu, click My Pages (or My Files).
  2. On the Pages tab, double-click a page to open it, or click + to make a new one. On the Files tab, you can see every file on your site, upload new ones, and delete old ones.

Notes

  • Your site's pages also appear as tabs above the picture. Click a tab to switch pages, or + to make a new one. Your changes to each page are kept as you switch, and nothing goes on the web until you publish.
  • The Site tab shows your site's address, when it was made and updated, and how much it holds. Site Style edits the stylesheet that every page of your site uses.
  • To see earlier saves of a page, select it on the Pages tab, and then click Versions. The last ten saves are kept, for when you have regrets.
  • Site Stats on the My Site menu shows who's viewing and editing your site right now.
  • To see your published page, on the My Site menu, click Open Live Page.
