To control Paint with your voice

  1. On the Extras menu, click Speech Recognition. Allow Paint to use your microphone when your browser asks.
  2. Say the name of a tool, a color, or a menu command. For example, say "brush", "red", or "undo".

Notes

  • You can also say things like "scroll up" or "zoom in".
  • Speech Recognition is available only in browsers that support it. If the command is grayed out, your browser doesn't.
  • To stop, click Speech Recognition again, or say "turn off speech recognition". Paint will stop listening. It was never judging. That's the assistant's job.
