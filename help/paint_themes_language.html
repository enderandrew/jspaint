To change how Paint looks

  • To change Paint's look, on the Extras menu, point to Themes, and then click a theme: Classic Light, Classic Dark, Modern Light, Modern Dark, Winter, or Bubblegum.
  • To change the language, on the Extras menu, point to Language, and then click a language. Some newer commands may stay in English. They're still learning.
  • To make the whole window fill the screen, on the View menu, click Fullscreen.
