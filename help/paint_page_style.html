To set the page's background, size, and column

  1. In the toolbox, click . (Or, on the Page menu, click Page Properties.)
  2. Set the Background color, Text color, and Wallpaper (a tiled image from your site, or an address). Click the color swatches to pick colors.
  3. To set where sections stack, type the Sections column position and width. Leave them empty for the standard column.
  4. Click OK.

Notes

  • The page itself is the picture. The background color and wallpaper show around it on the published page, and around the canvas while you work.
  • To change how wide the page is, on the Page menu, point to Page Width, and then click Phone, Classic (800 pixels, the width of the web in 1999), or Fit This Screen.
  • To make the page longer, click the button under the picture, or on the Page menu, click Make Page Longer. Each click adds 300 pixels. Make Page Shorter takes them back.
