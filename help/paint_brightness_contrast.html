To change the brightness or contrast of a picture

  1. If you want to change only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the Adjustments menu, click Brightness/Contrast.
  3. Drag the Brightness and Contrast sliders. The picture changes as you drag, so you can see the result.
  4. Click OK to keep the change, or Cancel to put the picture back the way it was.

Notes

  • To compare the change with the original, click the Preview check box to turn the preview off and on.
  • Moving a slider to the right makes the picture brighter or more contrasty; to the left, darker or flatter. This is the one part of this Help that is not a joke.
  • The change is one step that you can undo.
