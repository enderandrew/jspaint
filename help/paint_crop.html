To crop a picture to a selection

  1. In the toolbox, click or , and select the part of the picture you want to keep.
  2. On the Image menu, click Crop to Selection (or press CTRL+SHIFT+X).

Notes

  • The picture becomes the size of the selection's box. Everything outside it is gone, until you undo.
  • If you've moved the selection, it's put down where it is first, and the picture is cropped around it there.
  • Page elements, such as text and GIFs on a web page, stay where they are on the page.
