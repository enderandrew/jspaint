To go back to an earlier version of a page

  1. On the Edit or My Site menu, click Page History.
  2. Click a version to see what the page looked like then.
  3. Click Go to this version.

Notes

  • Page History shows every change to the page by everyone who edited it, as a timeline or a tree, with who did what and when.
  • Going back doesn't throw anything away. The later versions stay, as a branch you can go back to.
  • Page History needs a page from your site. For a picture that isn't on a site, use History on the Edit menu, which shows this copy's own undo history.
