To write blog posts

  1. On the My Site menu, click My Pages, and then click New Post.
  2. Type a title, and pick the folder for your posts. Paint makes the post with a title section and today's date.
  3. Write the post, and then publish it.

Notes

  • A post is just a page in a posts folder. Posts folders get an RSS feed automatically, at folder/feed.xml, for both of your subscribers.
  • To list your posts on another page (your front page, say), put a Folder View on it, and pick the folder.
