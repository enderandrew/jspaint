To read the Terms of Service

On the Help menu, click Terms of Service.

Notes

  • When the Terms of Service are updated, they appear when you open Paint. To agree, scroll all the way to the bottom, and then click I Agree.
  • You can also click I Disagree. Your disagreement is noted, and counts as agreeing.
  • The Terms of Service are not a real legal agreement. They're barely a real document.
