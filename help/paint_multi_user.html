To draw together with other people

  1. On the Extras menu, point to Multi-User, and then click New Session From Document to share the picture you have open, or New Blank Session to start with a blank one.
  2. Type a name for the session, and then click Start.
  3. Send the link Paint shows you to the people you want to draw with. Or just tell them the name.

To join someone's session

  1. On the Extras menu, point to Multi-User, and then click Join Session.
  2. Type the session's name (or paste its link), and then click Join.

Notes

  • Everyone sees the same picture, and everyone else's mouse pointer, in their own color.
  • To go back to drawing on your own, click Leave Session. You keep a copy of the picture.
  • Sessions are public. Anyone who knows (or guesses) the name can see and change the picture, so don't share anything you'd mind strangers drawing a mustache on.
  • It isn't seamless: when someone else draws, it can interrupt what you're in the middle of. Taking turns works best. So does not drawing mustaches.
  • Only the picture is shared, not page elements such as text boxes and GIFs. To edit a web page with other people, see To edit a page with other people.
