To show or hide the Paint Assistant

On the AI menu, click Show Assistant. A check mark appears when the assistant is showing.

Notes

  • The assistant offers suggestions while you work: when you pick a tool, undo several times in a row, paint for a while, or sit still for too long. The suggestions are funny. They are not helpful.
  • To get a tip whenever you want one, click the assistant. To move it, drag it. To close a tip, click the × in its corner.
  • Some suggestions offer a choice. Some of the choices open other AI features; the rest just lead to more suggestions.
  • The assistant waits a while between suggestions, and only gives each one once per visit. It's trying to grow.
  • If you hide the assistant, Paint remembers, and it stays hidden the next time you open Paint.
  • Clicking Mental on the Image menu may send the assistant away for a while. It will be back.
