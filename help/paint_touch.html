To use Paint on a phone or tablet

  • Draw with one finger. To scroll or zoom, use two fingers.
  • With the Pointer tool, dragging an empty part of the page with one finger scrolls it.
  • The joystick at the bottom right also scrolls the page. To show or hide it, on the View menu, click Pan Joystick.
  • The Undo and Redo buttons at the bottom right are always there, because shaking your phone doesn't undo anything.
  • To make the page as wide as a phone screen, on the Page menu, point to Page Width, and then click Phone.
