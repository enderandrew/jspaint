To let the AI paint a masterpiece (AI Mode)

AI Mode turns your brush strokes into a priceless work of art. Results are guaranteed, because the art was already there.

  1. On the AI menu, click AI Mode. A new blank canvas opens, sized to fit the painting the AI has in mind.
  2. Paint with any tool: the Brush, Pencil, shapes, the Fill With Color tool, even the Eraser and Text. Wherever you paint, the masterpiece appears.
  3. To get a different painting, on the AI menu, click New AI Masterpiece.
  4. To leave AI Mode, on the AI menu, click AI Mode again. Any areas you didn't paint become white, and your colors come back.

Notes

  • There is no AI. The painting is hidden under the canvas from the start, in the files images/ai-1.jpg, images/ai-2.jpg, and so on. Add your own pictures (JPEG, PNG, or WebP) with the next number to give the AI new ideas. The numbers have to be in order: the AI stops looking at the first missing one, like it does with most things.
  • Picking a color from the color box has no effect in AI Mode. The AI has strong opinions about color.
  • The Fill With Color tool reveals a large area at once, which the AI considers a collaboration.
  • When you save, unpainted areas are saved as white.
  • Opening or creating another picture ends AI Mode right away.
  • You can undo any step, as usual. The AI will not take it personally.
