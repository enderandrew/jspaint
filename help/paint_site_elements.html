To add a guestbook, counter, or other site element

Some elements are filled in by your site when someone visits the published page. In Paint, they show what your site shows right now.

  1. In the toolbox, click the element's tool.
  2. Click the page, or drag a box the size you want.
  3. To change its settings, double-click it.

Notes

  • Guestbook: visitors can sign it on the published page, and your site keeps the entries. Set its title and how many entries to show.
  • Visitor Counter: counts up with each visit. Set its label and the number of digits, for that authentic odometer look.
  • Last Updated: shows when the page was last published, in a short or long date.
  • Folder View: lists the pages in a folder of your site, such as your blog posts, newest first. Pick the folder, what to show, the order, and how many.
  • Contents: lists this page's sections, each a link to it.
  • These elements need your site to fill them in. When you're not signed in, they show a placeholder instead.
