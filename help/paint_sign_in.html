To sign in, or make another site

  1. Click the globe at the bottom of the toolbox. (Or, on the My Site menu, click Sign In.)
  2. Click Sign in with Google, or type your site's name and its password.

Notes

  • An account can have up to five sites. To make another one, on the My Site menu, click New Site. Each site has its own address ending in /~name/.
  • When you're signed in, the globe shows your site's name. Click it to see your site's address, who's viewing and editing it right now, and your other sites. Click a site to switch to it.
  • To sign out, on the My Site menu, click Sign Out. Your drawing stays where it is.
