To select, move, and resize page elements

  1. In the toolbox, click .
  2. Click an element to select it. Drag it to move it, or drag its handles to resize it.
  3. To edit an element's text, double-click it (or press ENTER). Press ESC when you're done.

Notes

  • To select several elements, click and drag a box over them. Hold down SHIFT to add more. Drag any of them to move them all, use the arrow keys to nudge them, or press DELETE to remove them. Ctrl+A selects every element.
  • The arrow keys nudge the selected element one pixel at a time, for perfectionists.
  • With the Pointer tool, dragging an empty part of the page scrolls it, which is handy on a phone.
  • To put an element in front of or behind others, on the Page menu, click Bring Element Forward or Send Element Backward.
  • To turn an element into pixels, on the Page menu, click Flatten Element. It's drawn into the picture and is no longer an element. Flatten All Elements does all of them.
  • To remove an element, select it and press DELETE, or on the Page menu, click Delete Element.
  • Moving, resizing, and deleting elements can be undone, one step at a time, along with your painting.
