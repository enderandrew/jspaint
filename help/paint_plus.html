To upgrade to Paint+

  1. On the Help menu, click Upgrade to Paint+.
  2. Under Payment method, choose how you'd like to pay: with exposure, with exactly one pixel, or by watching a short ad.
  3. Under the plan you want, click Subscribe.

Notes

  • Paint+ includes everything in Paint, plus a badge. Paint+ Pro Max Ultra includes a bigger badge. No features are unlocked, because none were locked.
  • No money changes hands, and Paint never asks for payment details. Your bank is not involved, though it is amused.
  • To change or cancel your plan, on the Help menu, click Upgrade to Paint+ again. Canceling takes a few steps. This is traditional.
  • Painting with all the colors of the wind requires Paint+ Max, which is coming soon.
