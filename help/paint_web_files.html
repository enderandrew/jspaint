To open a picture from the web, or manage stored pictures

  • To open a picture from an address on the web, on the File menu, click Load From URL, and then type or paste the address.
  • To upload your picture to Imgur, on the File menu, click Upload To Imgur.
  • Paint keeps the pictures you've worked on in your browser, so they come back after a reload. To see them, open one, or delete the ones you don't need, on the File menu, click Manage Storage.
