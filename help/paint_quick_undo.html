To add a large Undo button

On the Extras menu, click Quick Undo Button.

Notes

  • A large Undo button appears in the corner of the window, so you can undo with one click, without the menu or the keyboard.
  • There are also small Undo and Redo buttons at the bottom right of the window, next to the color box. Those are always there.
  • To remove the large button, click Quick Undo Button again.
