To put a page on the web

  1. Click Publish at the top right of the window. (Or, on the File or My Site menu, click Publish.)
  2. If you're not signed in, sign in with Google, or with your site's name and password.
  3. If you don't have a site yet, type a name for it. The name becomes your site's address, so choose wisely, or at least memorably.
  4. When the page is up, Paint shows its address, with buttons to open it, copy the link, or share it.

Notes

  • After a page has been published once, press Ctrl+S to publish your changes.
  • Site names can have lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens, up to 32 characters.
  • A dot on the Publish button means the page has changes that aren't on the web yet.
  • Your site's front page is index.html. Every page is also at a short address without the .html.
  • To make your own "page not found" page, make a page named 404. Visitors who get lost will see it instead of the default.
  • Published pages have no scripts and no trackers. What you put there is what visitors get.
