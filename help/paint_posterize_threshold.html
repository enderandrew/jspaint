To reduce a picture to fewer colors

  1. If you want to change only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the Adjustments menu, click Posterize or Threshold.
  3. Drag the slider until the picture looks the way you want, and then click OK.

Notes

  • Posterize limits each color channel to the number of Levels you choose. Fewer levels make bolder, flatter areas of color, like a poster.
  • Threshold turns the picture into pure black and white. Parts lighter than the Level you choose become white; the rest become black. It's very decisive.
  • The change is one step that you can undo.
