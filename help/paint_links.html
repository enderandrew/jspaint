To add a link

  1. Select the element you want to be a link (a picture, a GIF, web text, or a text box) with the Pointer tool. Or, while editing a section, select the words.
  2. In the toolbox, click . (Or, on the Page menu, click Add Link to Element or Insert Link, or press Ctrl+K while writing.)
  3. Type an address, click one of your pages, or pick a section, and then click OK.

Notes

  • To remove a link, open the link dialog again and clear the address.
  • Your pages appear as thumbnails, so you can link to them without remembering what you named them.
  • Counters, guestbooks, tables, and dividers can't be links. They have enough going on.
