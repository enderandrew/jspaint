To use the novelty adjustments

The bottom of the Adjustments menu has adjustments you won't find in other programs, for reasons that will become clear.

  1. If you want to change only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the Adjustments menu, click one of the commands below.

Notes

  • Make It Pop raises the contrast and saturation. The Pop slider describes how popping your picture is, from "Not popping" to "Exploded."
  • Attitude doesn't change your picture. The Paint Assistant coaches you through adjusting your attitude instead, which is where the problem was.
  • Deep Fry oversaturates, oversharpens, and browns your picture, then saves it as a very low-quality JPEG three times. Serve immediately.
  • 1998 Film Filter gives your picture the warm, faded, grainy look of a disposable camera, with an orange date stamp in the corner.
  • Add Lens Flare adds a lens flare, as was required by law in the late 1990s.
  • Fix Everything examines your picture very carefully and fixes everything that is wrong with it. So far, it has never found anything.
  • Each of these is one step that you can undo, except Attitude, which you'll have to undo yourself.
