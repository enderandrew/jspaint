To generate a picture from a description

  1. If you want the picture to go in one part of your picture, select that part first. Otherwise it fills the whole picture.
  2. On the AI menu, click Prompt.
  3. Describe the picture you want, and then press ENTER or click Generate. (To start a new line instead, press SHIFT+ENTER.)
  4. Watch the preview as the AI "denoises" your picture. When it's done, the picture appears on the canvas, and the AI comments on your prompt.

Notes

  • Whatever you describe, the result is one of the AI Mode paintings. The AI takes creative liberties. All of them.
  • The same description always gives the same picture, so you can reproduce your results exactly, for what that's worth.
  • Adding words such as "4k" or "highly detailed" improves results by 0%.
  • Ask for a paperclip and you'll get the Paint Assistant, who was hoping you'd ask.
  • If there are no AI Mode paintings, the AI draws exactly what you asked for: your words.
  • The Prompt box stays open, so you can keep trying. Generating is one step that you can undo.
