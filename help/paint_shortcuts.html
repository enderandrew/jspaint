Mouse and keyboard tricks

  • To draw a straight line at an exact angle, hold down SHIFT while you draw with the Line or Curve tool. It snaps to horizontal, vertical, or 45 degrees.
  • To draw a perfect square or circle, hold down SHIFT while you draw with the Rectangle, Ellipse, or Rounded Rectangle tool.
  • To keep a selection's proportions while you resize it, hold down SHIFT while you drag one of its corner handles.
  • To cancel a stroke while you're still drawing, press the other mouse button. The stroke disappears, and nobody has to know.
  • Left-click draws with the foreground color, and right-click draws with the background color. Most tools work with both.
  • To make the brush, eraser, airbrush, pencil, or line thicker or thinner, press + or − (on the number pad, or the = and − keys). With a selection, + and − double or halve its size.
  • To cut the picture down to the selection, press CTRL+SHIFT+X, or on the Image menu, click Crop to Selection.
  • To zoom to 100%, press CTRL+PAGE UP. To zoom to 400%, press CTRL+PAGE DOWN.
  • To rotate the picture (or a selected sticker) a quarter turn, press CTRL+. (period) or CTRL+, (comma).
