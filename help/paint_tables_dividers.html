To add a table or a divider

  1. In the toolbox, click or .
  2. Click the page, or drag a box the size you want.
  3. For a table, click in a cell to type.

Notes

  • To change a table's background color, border, or cell padding, or a divider's thickness and color, select it and then, on the Page menu, click Element Properties.
  • To add rows or columns, use Edit Element HTML on the Page menu. Tables have always been like this.
