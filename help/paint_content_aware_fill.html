To remove something with Content-Aware Fill

  1. In the toolbox, click or , and then select the thing you want to remove.
  2. On the AI menu, click Content-Aware Fill.
  3. When the Paint Assistant asks if you would like help, click either answer. They do the same thing.

Notes

  • The selected area really is filled in from the colors around it, so the thing you selected is gone. Then the Paint Assistant moves into the empty space, to help.
  • Content-Aware Fill uses the picture images/clippy.jpg for the assistant. Without it, Content-Aware Fill can't find its assistant and does nothing.
  • A free-form selection keeps its shape.
  • Content-Aware Fill is one step that you can undo, which also evicts the assistant.
