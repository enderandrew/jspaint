Other commands

A few commands in the menus don't do anything to your picture. They're here for your other needs.

  • File > Taxes prepares and files your taxes. You will not get a refund.
  • Image > Mental connects you with your mental imagery. If the Paint Assistant is showing, it sees your mental imagery too, and leaves for a while to talk to someone about it.
  • Adjustments > Attitude is a short coaching session with the Paint Assistant. Click Next for each step.
  • Page > Doctor pages a doctor to the OR.
  • Colors > Of the Wind would let you paint with all the colors of the wind, if you had Paint+ Max.
  • Help > Send sends help. Help may have some news for you.
