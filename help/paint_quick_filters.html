To apply a one-click filter

  1. If you want to change only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the Adjustments menu, click Auto Contrast, Grayscale, Sepia, Emboss, or Find Edges.

Notes

  • Auto Contrast stretches the colors so the darkest parts become black and the lightest become white. It fixes dull, foggy pictures. It does not fix anything else.
  • Grayscale removes the color. Sepia gives the picture the brown tint of an old photograph.
  • Emboss makes the picture look raised from a gray surface. Find Edges turns it into dark outlines on white, like a coloring book.
  • These filters have no settings. They know what they're doing.
  • Each filter is one step that you can undo.
