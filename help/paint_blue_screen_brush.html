To paint with the Blue Screen Brush

  1. On the Colors menu, click Blue Screen Brush. The Brush tool is selected, with a large brush size.
  2. Paint on the picture. Your strokes are painted with the text of a blue screen error.
  3. Every so often, the brush itself crashes. To continue, press any key or click.
  4. To stop, on the Colors menu, click Blue Screen Brush again, or click a color in the color box.

Notes

  • The crash is not real, and nothing is lost. Your picture is exactly as you left it. Probably.
  • Other tools, such as the shapes and the Fill With Color tool, also paint with the blue screen while it's on.
  • When you stop, your previous colors and brush size come back.
