To enhance a picture

Like on TV, you can zoom in on a picture and enhance it.

  1. If you want to enhance only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the AI menu, click Enhance!
  3. Wait while the AI upscales your picture. To stop, click Cancel.

Notes

  • Unlike on TV, enhancing makes your picture blockier. The status bar will tell you how much detail was added. It's lying.
  • Each time you enhance the same picture, the blocks get bigger: 4, 8, 16, 32, and then 64 pixels across.
  • Enhancing is one step that you can undo, which is the recommended way to enhance.
