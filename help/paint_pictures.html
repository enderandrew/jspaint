To add pictures and photos

  1. In the toolbox, click .
  2. Click a picture to put it on the page, or click Upload to add a new one from your computer.

Notes

  • If you're signed in, pictures are kept on your site right away. If you're not, they're kept in the page until you publish it.
  • Big photos are shown at page size and link to the full-size original, so your page loads quickly and your photos still look sharp.
  • GIF, PNG, JPEG, and WebP pictures are welcome. Other formats are politely turned away.
  • If you're writing in a section, the picture goes into the text instead of floating on the page.
