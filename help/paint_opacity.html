To make a picture see-through

  1. If you want to change only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the Adjustments menu, click Opacity.
  3. Drag the Opacity slider to the left to make the picture more see-through. 100% is solid; 0% is invisible.
  4. Click OK to keep the change, or Cancel to put the picture back the way it was.

Notes

  • For the whole picture, the document becomes transparent (see Attributes on the Image menu). To keep the see-through parts when you save, save as PNG, GIF, TIFF, or a web page; most other formats fill them in.
  • For a selection, the selection shows the picture under it when you put it down, like tracing paper.
  • The change is one step that you can undo. Lowering the opacity again makes it fainter still; raising it doesn't bring back what's gone.
