To change the colors of a picture

  1. If you want to change only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the Adjustments menu, click Hue/Saturation.
  3. Drag the sliders: Hue shifts every color around the color wheel, Saturation makes colors more or less vivid, and Lightness makes everything lighter or darker.
  4. Click OK to keep the change, or Cancel to put things back.

Notes

  • Shifting the hue by 180° turns each color into its opposite, but keeps light and dark the same (unlike Invert Colors on the Image menu).
  • Moving Saturation all the way to the left gives you black and white. Moving it all the way to the right gives you the 1990s.
  • The change is one step that you can undo.
