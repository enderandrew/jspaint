To put text on a page

  1. In the toolbox, click .
  2. Click the page where you want the text, or drag a box the size you want.
  3. Type your text. Use the Font toolbar to pick a font, size, bold, italic, or underline, and click a color in the color box to color the selected words.
  4. Press ESC, or click somewhere else, when you're done.

Notes

  • For a heading, use a bigger font size. There's no separate heading tool; it's just text that's shouting.
  • To make the text scroll across its box, click the Marquee button on the Font toolbar while you're editing it. Click it again to make it stop scrolling. Visitors may thank you.
  • The fonts on the Font toolbar are the classic web fonts (Arial, Comic Sans MS, Times New Roman, and friends), so the page looks the same on your visitors' computers.
  • The Web Text tool works like Paint's Text tool, but when you finish, the words stay text instead of becoming pixels. You can double-click them later to change them, and make them a link.
  • The regular Text tool still paints text into the picture as pixels, as it did in 1998.
