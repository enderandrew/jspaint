To control Paint with your head or by hovering

  1. On the Extras menu, click Head Tracker. Allow Paint to use your camera when your browser asks.
  2. In the Tracky Mouse window, click Start (or press F9). Move your head to move the pointer.
  3. To click, hold the pointer still over a button or menu. After a moment, it clicks for you.

Notes

  • To click by hovering without using the camera, on the Extras menu, click Dwell Clicker instead. (Head Tracker includes it.)
  • Use the sliders in the Tracky Mouse window to change how fast the pointer moves.
  • To pause dwell clicking, click the eye button at the bottom of the window.
  • Head tracking runs in your browser; the camera picture isn't sent anywhere.
  • Head Tracker and Dwell Clicker are accessibility features, and they are not jokes. Everything else on this menu is negotiable.
