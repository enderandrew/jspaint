To see the history of your picture

On the Edit menu, click History.

Notes

  • History shows every change to the picture, including ones you undid and then replaced with other changes. Click any step to go back to it. Nothing is ever really lost, which is more than can be said for 1998.
  • To make an animated GIF of your picture being made, on the Extras menu, click Render History As GIF.
