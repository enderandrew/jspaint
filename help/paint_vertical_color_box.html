To arrange the color box vertically

On the Extras menu, click Vertical Color Box.

Notes

  • The color box moves to the right side of the window, in a tall column. The Undo and Redo buttons move with it.
  • This leaves more room for your picture on wide screens, and makes the colors easier to reach with head tracking.
  • To put the color box back at the bottom, click Vertical Color Box again.
