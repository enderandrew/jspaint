To blur, sharpen, or add noise to a picture

  1. If you want to change only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the Adjustments menu, click Blur, Sharpen, or Add Noise.
  3. Adjust the settings while you watch the preview, and then click OK.

Notes

  • Blur softens the picture. A bigger Radius blurs more.
  • Sharpen makes edges crisper. Amount sets how much; Radius sets how wide the edges are. Too much sharpening makes halos, which some people consider a style.
  • Add Noise adds grain. Click Color noise for colored speckles instead of gray ones.
  • Each change is one step that you can undo.
