To edit a page with other people

  1. Open the page from your site.
  2. On the My Site menu, click Share Page.
  3. Send the link (or show the QR code) to the people you want to edit with.

Notes

  • Anyone with the link can draw on that page with you right away, without signing in. They can't change your other pages.
  • Everyone sees everyone else's changes as they happen, along with their pointers and names. To set your name, click the live editing indicator on the status bar.
  • To stop old links from working, click Revoke Links in the Share dialog.
  • Live editing keeps a page you're editing the same everywhere you sign in. To turn it off, on the View menu, click Live Sync.
  • Undo only undoes your own changes, so nobody can undo you. Not even you, if you're someone else.
