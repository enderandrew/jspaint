To defragment your picture

  1. If you want to defragment only part of the picture, select that part first.
  2. On the Image menu, click Defragment Canvas.
  3. Watch the clusters move into place. To stop, click Stop; your picture won't change.

Notes

  • Defragmenting really does rearrange every pixel in your picture: grouped by color, grays first, then each hue from dark to light, with white "free space" at the end. No pixels are lost. They're just very well organized now.
  • Defragmenting does not make your picture faster. You may notice a slight change in appearance.
  • Like the original, it stops at 99% for a while. This is normal.
  • Defragmenting is one step that you can undo.
